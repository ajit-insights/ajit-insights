# Open source software I use

Most of what I build and use every day stands on open source. I pick tools that work offline, keep my data in files I own, and are easy to back up. This is what I actually run.

*Last reviewed: 21-11-2026*

---

## On my desktop and phone

1. **Operating system**: [Linux Mint](https://linuxmint.com/) with the KDE Plasma desktop
2. **Web browsers**: [Firefox](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/), [Chromium](https://www.chromium.org/) and [Brave](https://brave.com/)
3. **Email**: [Proton Mail](https://protonmail.com) and [Tuta Mail](https://tuta.com)
4. **Messengers**: [Signal](https://signal.org/download/), [Jami](https://jami.net/) and [materialgram](https://github.com/kukuruzka165/materialgram), a desktop Telegram client
5. **Office suite**: [LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org/)
6. **Passwords**: [Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com/) clients on my own [Vaultwarden](https://github.com/dani-garcia/vaultwarden) server, and [KeePass](https://keepass.info/) for local
7. **Encryption**: [Cryptomator](https://cryptomator.org/)
8. **Backups and sync**: [Déjà Dup](https://apps.gnome.org/en-GB/DejaDup/), [rclone](https://rclone.org/) and [Syncthing](https://syncthing.net/)
9. **Private network**: [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com/) (the client is open source)
10. **Video**: [FreeTube](https://freetubeapp.io/)

## Notes, knowledge and learning

11. **Notes**: [Standard Notes](https://standardnotes.com/) for online sync and [Markor](https://github.com/gsantner/markor) for local
12. **Knowledge graph**: [Logseq](https://logseq.com/)
13. **Mind maps**: [Freeplane](https://www.freeplane.org/)
14. **E-books and library**: [Calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com/download) and [Koodo Reader](https://www.koodoreader.com/en)
15. **References**: [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org/)
16. **Memory cards**: [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/), with its own sync server

## Tasks and money

17. **To-do**: [Super Productivity](https://super-productivity.com/), [Orgzly](https://www.orgzly.com/) (text based) and [Tasks.org](https://tasks.org/) (UI based)
18. **Projects**: [Plane](https://plane.so/), self-hosted
19. **Wiki**: [Outline](https://www.getoutline.com/), self-hosted
20. **Money**: [Money Manager Ex](https://moneymanagerex.org/)

## On my home server

21. **Documents**: [Paperless-ngx](https://docs.paperless-ngx.com/) with [Apache Tika](https://tika.apache.org/) and [Gotenberg](https://gotenberg.dev/)
22. **Notifications**: [ntfy](https://ntfy.sh/)
23. **Databases**: [SQLite](https://sqlite.org/), [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/), [DuckDB](https://duckdb.org/) and [Litestream](https://litestream.io/) for replication
24. **Reading scans**: [Tesseract](https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract) OCR, [Poppler](https://poppler.freedesktop.org/) and [Ghostscript](https://ghostscript.com/)
25. **Files and safety**: [Samba](https://www.samba.org/), [fail2ban](https://github.com/fail2ban/fail2ban) and [smartmontools](https://www.smartmontools.org/)
