# Open source software I use

Most of what I build and use every day stands on open source. I pick tools that work offline, keep my data in files I own, and are easy to back up. This is what I actually use.

*Last reviewed: 21-11-2026*

---

## Everyday

1. **Operating system**: [Fedora Linux](https://fedoraproject.org/)
2. **Web browsers**: [Firefox](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/), [Chromium](https://www.chromium.org/) and [Brave](https://brave.com/)
3. **Email**: [Proton Mail](https://protonmail.com) and [Tuta Mail](https://tuta.com)
4. **Messenger**: [Signal](https://signal.org/download/)
5. **Office suite**: [LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org/)
6. **Password managers**: [Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com/) for online sync and [KeePass](https://keepass.info/) for local
7. **Backups**: [Déjà Dup](https://apps.gnome.org/en-GB/DejaDup/) and [rclone](https://rclone.org/)
8. **File sync**: [Syncthing](https://syncthing.net/)

## Notes, knowledge and learning

9. **Notes**: [Standard Notes](https://standardnotes.com/) for online sync and [Markor](https://github.com/gsantner/markor) for local
10. **Knowledge graph**: [Logseq](https://logseq.com/)
11. **Mind maps**: [Freeplane](https://www.freeplane.org/)
12. **E-books and library**: [Calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com/download) and [Koodo Reader](https://www.koodoreader.com/en)
13. **Documents**: [Paperless-ngx](https://docs.paperless-ngx.com/)
14. **References**: [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org/)
15. **Memory cards**: [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/)

## Tasks and money

16. **To-do**: [Orgzly](https://www.orgzly.com/) (text based) and [Tasks.org](https://tasks.org/) (UI based)
17. **Projects**: [Plane](https://plane.so/)
18. **Wiki**: [Outline](https://www.getoutline.com/)
19. **Money**: [Money Manager Ex](https://moneymanagerex.org/)

## Data and plumbing

20. **Database**: [SQLite](https://sqlite.org/)
21. **Languages**: [Python](https://www.python.org/), [R](https://www.r-project.org/) and [Kotlin](https://kotlinlang.org/)
22. **Notifications**: [ntfy](https://ntfy.sh/)
